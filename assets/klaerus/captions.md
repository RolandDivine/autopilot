## LinkedIn
I built a trading app that shows you the answer first.

Most crypto trading runs on leverage. You pick 20× because everyone does, and you find out what it meant when the position is gone and you've lost more than you planned to.

Klærus works the other way round. Before you commit anything, the screen shows you:
→ the exact payout ("$250 → up to $687.50")
→ the exact price where the trade ends, drawn as a red line on the chart
→ that your stake is the most you can lose: no borrowing, no margin call, no negative balance

Mid-trade you can cut profit or stop out at a quoted price. You're never locked in.

The part I'm proudest of: it publishes its own house edge. Every payout sits a little below fair value, because that's how the platform makes money, and the app lists that margin per contract, generated from the live settings rather than written by hand.

Where it stands, honestly: it's a paper-trading demo. $1,000 of play money, no sign-up, no real deposits. Prices come from the live market.
