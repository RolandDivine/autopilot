## LinkedIn text
In October 2025, a 32GB memory kit cost $117. A few months later: $439.

That's not a supply glitch. It's AI.

Data centers are expected to use about 70% of all memory chips made this year (IDC). Chip makers sell to AI companies first because they pay more, and consumer devices get what's left.

The knock-on effects:
→ Memory contract prices rose up to 95% in a single quarter (TrendForce)
→ The average smartphone is expected to cost 14% more in 2026 (IDC)
→ Phones under $100 could become "permanently uneconomical" (IDC)
→ Phone sales in the Middle East and Africa could fall more than 20% (IDC)

And it won't end soon. IDC expects prices to calm around mid-2027; SK Hynix's CEO says the shortage could last past 2030.

The AI boom isn't just a story about chatbots. It's showing up in the price of the device in your pocket.

If you're buying a phone or laptop this year: buy the storage you'll need for years, and look seriously at certified refurbished.
