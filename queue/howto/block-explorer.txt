Hey, Kobo here. Every crypto transaction is public. A block explorer lets you see it for yourself. Here's how to use one, in six steps.

Step one. Pick the right explorer for the network. Etherscan for Ethereum. Mempool dot space for Bitcoin. Solscan for Solana.

Step two. Paste in a transaction hash, the long ID your wallet gives you after you send something.

Step three. Check the status. Success, pending, or failed. If someone says they sent you money, this is where you check, not a screenshot.

Step four. Read the details. From, to, amount, and the fee you paid. Make sure the to address is exactly the one you meant.

Step five. Paste in a wallet address to see its balance and history. Useful for checking a project's wallet, or your own.

Step six. Check token approvals. On Etherscan, the token approval checker shows which apps can spend your tokens. Remove the ones you don't use.

Everything is public, so never share a wallet you want to keep private. Education, not financial advice. Subscribe for the next how-to.
