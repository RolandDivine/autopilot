Hey, Kobo here. Every crypto chart is made of candles. Once you can read one, the chart stops looking like noise. Two minutes, six steps.

Step one. One candle is one slice of time. On the four-hour chart, each candle is four hours of trading.

Step two. The body. It shows where price opened and where it closed. Green means it closed higher than it opened. Red means it closed lower.

Step three. The wicks. Those thin lines above and below show the highest and lowest prices reached. A long wick means price went there, but got pushed back.

Step four. Size tells you strength. A big body means one side was in control. A tiny body with long wicks means buyers and sellers were fighting, and nobody won.

Step five. Context beats patterns. A long lower wick at a level you marked means more than the same candle in the middle of nowhere.

Step six. Zoom out. Check the daily candle before you judge a four-hour one.
