Hey, Kobo here. Before you buy any coin, spend two minutes on CoinGecko. It's free, and it can save you from a bad decision. Six steps.

Step one. Go to coingecko.com and search the coin's name. Check you've got the right one. Many fake coins copy famous names.

Step two. Look at market cap, not just price. A coin at one cent isn't cheap if there are trillions of them. Market cap is price times supply.

Step three. Check the supply. Circulating supply is what's out now. Max supply is what could exist. A big gap means more coins may hit the market later.

Step four. Check the trading volume. Very low volume means it can be hard to sell without moving the price.

Step five. Open the markets tab. Is it traded on big, well-known exchanges, or only on one small one?

Step six. Read the contract address and official links, and copy the contract only from there, never from a random post.

Two minutes of homework beats months of regret. Education, not financial advice. Subscribe for more.
