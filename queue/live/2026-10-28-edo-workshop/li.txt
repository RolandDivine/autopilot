Today I was in the room at the Edo State Annual Development Stakeholders Workshop 2026, as a delegate representing youth affairs.

The theme: Enhancing Collaboration and Coordination in Development Partnerships.

Government, development partners and agencies at one table, planning for 2027. The agenda asked three honest questions:
What is working well?
What is slowing us down?
What must we do differently?

My focus is young people, on three fronts:

1. Skill acquisition that leads to real work, not just certificates.
2. Empowerment that lasts after the programme ends.
3. Migration: making building a future at home a real choice, and making sure those who travel do it safely and legally.

Here's what I believe: young Nigerians don't lack talent or hunger. What's often missing is coordination. Too many programmes run side by side, each starting from zero. When partners plan together, one programme can pick up where another stops.

So I want to carry your voice into this conversation.
If you're a young person in Edo, or anywhere in Nigeria: what's the one thing that would make a skills programme actually change your life?

Tell me in the comments. I'll take your answers into the 2027 planning conversations.
