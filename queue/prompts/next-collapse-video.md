## 2. How the video must work

**a. 0–2 seconds is the question.** The very first spoken words are the hook question. The same question fills the screen as text, so it works with the sound off. There's no intro, no logo and no "hey guys".

**b. Triple hook by 7 seconds:**
1. **Context:** the question, with "financial collapse".
2. **Lean:** "2000, 2008, 2020: the same warning lights came on first."
3. **Snapback:** "I checked them against today. Five are on."

**c. Recurring device: the WARNING DASHBOARD.** This is a 5x3 grid of 15 round lights, introduced at about 4 seconds. Each "light" segment zooms into one light, then returns to the dashboard as that light turns red. At the end, the dashboard shows 5 red, 7 amber and 3 grey.
- This device is the "signature" and makes the video visually different from the series Reels.
- It also builds the open loop: viewers stay to see the full board.

**d. Peaks every 6–10 seconds.** Each light is one peak: a number that ticks up, a year stamp (2000 / 2008 / 2020) next to "NOW", and a sound hit.

**e. The twist (around 42s):** the honest "but here's what's missing" beat. This builds trust and sets up the verdict.

**f. Open loop:**
- Plant it early: "and one date nobody's watching" (at about 7s).
- Close it at the end with **30 November**.

**g. Educative CTA:** an on-screen "watch list" of 3 signals to track, then a comment keyword.

## 4. Visual style

**Same base as trump_swan.py:**
- background #0b0b0f, white Jakarta bold;
- Lora Italic for the accent word;
- Roland's avatar and "Roland Divine · @defi_ring_master" at the top left;
- a yellow progress bar at the bottom.

**Colours:**
- red = light on / danger;
- amber (#ffc83d) = partly on;
- grey (#3a3a42) = off;
- yellow = numbers and the progress bar.
- Use at most two accent colours per frame.

**Dashboard:**
- 15 circles of 64px in a 5x3 grid, centred at y≈700.
- A light turning red gets a glow and a low "thump".
- The dashboard returns between lights, so viewers feel the board filling up.

**Motion:**
- something moves or changes every 1.5–2 seconds;
- counters tick up;
- year stamps slam in with a slight shake;
- charts draw on with stroke animation.

**Captions:** word by word, lower third. Colour the keywords: collapse, 2000, 2008, 2020, five, forty percent, borrowed money, trillion, riskiest, twelve percent, Bitcoin, Congress, missing, Yet, November thirtieth, SIGNALS.

**Sound:**
- a whoosh on every return to the dashboard;
- an impact on each "light on";
- ticks on counters;
- a pop on the CTA;
- a low, tense bed under the voice.

**Imagery rules:**
- No real faces, politicians, logos, seals or exchange brands.
- No celebrity or cloned voice.
