Hey, Kobo here. Every four years or so, Bitcoin does something no central bank does. It cuts its own money printing in half.
New bitcoin is created as a reward for miners, the computers that secure the network. Every two hundred and ten thousand blocks, roughly four years, that reward halves.
In 2009 it was fifty bitcoin per block. Then twenty-five. Then twelve and a half. Then six point two five. In April 2024 it dropped to three point one two five. The next halving is expected around 2028.
Why does it matter? Fewer new coins come into the market each day. That's why there will never be more than twenty-one million.
But careful. Past halvings came before big price runs, and people treat that like a rule. It isn't. Markets change, and the halving is known years in advance.
So learn how it works. Don't bet your rent on a pattern. See ya.
