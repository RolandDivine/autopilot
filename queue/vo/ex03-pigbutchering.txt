Hey, Kobo here. It starts with a wrong-number text. Sorry, wrong person. But they're friendly, so you keep chatting.
Weeks pass. They become a friend, sometimes more. Then, casually, they mention a crypto trading app that's making them rich. They show you screenshots. They offer to teach you.
You deposit a little. The app shows profits. You deposit more. Then you try to withdraw, and suddenly there are taxes, fees, a verification charge. The money never comes out. The app was fake from day one.
Scammers call it pig butchering: fatten the victim with trust, then take everything.
In its 2024 report, the FBI said Americans lost about nine point three billion dollars to crypto fraud, and investment scams like this were the biggest share.
The rule is simple. Never invest through an app or a person you met online. If you can't withdraw, stop sending. See ya.
