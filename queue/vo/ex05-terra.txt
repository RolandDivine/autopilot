Hey, Kobo here. In May 2022, a coin that was supposed to always be worth one dollar fell to almost zero in days. It was called UST.
Most stablecoins hold cash or bonds in reserve. UST didn't. It used an algorithm and a sister token, LUNA. One UST could always be swapped for one dollar's worth of LUNA.
It worked while people trusted it. A platform called Anchor paid close to twenty percent a year on UST, and billions flowed in.
Then big withdrawals hit. UST slipped below a dollar. People swapped UST for LUNA and sold. LUNA crashed, which made UST weaker, which made more people sell. A death spiral.
Tens of billions of dollars in value disappeared.
The lesson. If a yield looks too good, ask where it comes from. And stable is a promise. Check what's backing it. See ya.
