Hey, Kobo here. Your phone rings. It's your brother's voice. He's crying. He's in trouble and needs money right now. Don't tell Mum.
Except it isn't him.
AI can now copy a voice from a few seconds of audio. A video on social media is enough. Scammers use it to call families and businesses, pretending to be someone you trust.
The script always has the same parts. Urgency. Secrecy. A strange way to pay, like crypto, gift cards, or a new account.
How to beat it. Agree on a family code word that only you know. If a call feels off, hang up and call the person back on the number you already have. Ask a question only the real person could answer.
And remember, a familiar voice is no longer proof of who's speaking.
Slow down, verify, then act. Share this with your family. See ya.
