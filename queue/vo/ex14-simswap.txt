Hey, Kobo here. One phone call to your mobile network can let someone take over your bank, your email, and your crypto. It's called a SIM swap.
Here's how. A scammer collects your details: name, birthday, address, often from leaks or social media. Then they contact your network, pretend to be you, and ask to move your number to a new SIM.
Your phone loses signal. Their phone now gets your calls and texts, including every login code sent by SMS. They reset your passwords and empty your accounts.
How to protect yourself. Ask your network to add a PIN or a port-out lock to your account. Move important accounts from SMS codes to an authenticator app or a passkey. Keep your birthday and phone number off public profiles.
And if your phone suddenly says no service for no reason, call your network right away from another phone. See ya.
