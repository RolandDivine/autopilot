Hey, Kobo here. Starting a business? Here's what the numbers say.
According to the US Bureau of Labor Statistics, about one in five new businesses close within their first year. Roughly half are gone within five years.
That isn't to scare you. It's to help you be in the other half.
The common reasons. Running out of cash. Building something people don't want to pay for. Pricing too low to make a profit. And growing costs faster than sales.
Kobo's playbook. One: get a paying customer before you spend big. Two: know your numbers every week. How much came in, how much went out, what's left. Three: keep at least three months of costs in reserve. Four: start small, then grow what works.
Most businesses don't fail because the idea was bad. They fail because the money ran out before the idea had time to work. See ya.
