Hey, Kobo here. A new coin is going up fast. Everyone in the group chat is buying. You buy too. Then you try to sell, and you can't.
That's a honeypot. The token's code lets people buy but blocks them from selling. Only the creator can cash out.
A rug pull is similar. The team builds hype, collects money, then pulls the cash out of the trading pool, or dumps their huge share, and vanishes. The price goes to zero in minutes.
Warning signs. An anonymous team. A few wallets holding most of the supply. Liquidity that isn't locked. Big promises and pressure to buy now.
Before you touch a new token, check it on a free token scanner, look at the holders on a block explorer, and search the name plus scam.
And if the only reason you're buying is that it's going up, step back. See ya.
