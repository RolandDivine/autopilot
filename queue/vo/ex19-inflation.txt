Hey, Kobo here. Your money can shrink without you spending a single naira, dollar, or pound. That's inflation.
Inflation means prices rise over time. If inflation is ten percent this year, something that cost one thousand now costs eleven hundred. The money in your drawer is the same, but it buys less.
Why does it happen? Often because there's more money chasing the same goods, or because things cost more to make and move. Fuel, food, and exchange rates all play a part.
What can you do? First, know the number. Check your country's official inflation rate. Second, don't keep long-term savings in cash under the mattress. Look for savings that pay interest, ideally above inflation. Third, build skills that let you raise your income.
And be careful. Inflation fear is a favourite tool of scammers selling get-rich-quick schemes.
Protect your money, but protect it wisely. See ya.
