Hey, Kobo here. You're on YouTube and see a live stream. A famous tech boss is talking about crypto. On screen: send one bitcoin, get two back. Limited time.
It's fake. Every single time.
Scammers take over YouTube channels, then stream old interviews or AI deepfakes of well-known people. They add a QR code and a wallet address. The live chat is full of bots saying it worked for them.
Nobody, no company, and no celebrity will double your crypto. Once you send it, it's gone. Crypto transactions can't be reversed.
Spot it fast. Check the channel. Was it renamed recently? Is the real person's official channel streaming? Any promise to multiply your money is the scam itself.
If you see one, report the stream. And if someone you know shares it, tell them.
If it sounds too good to be true, it is. See ya.
