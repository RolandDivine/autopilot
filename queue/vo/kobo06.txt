Hey, it's Kobo. Season two. And we start with a mystery: where does your money go?
Ada gets paid on Friday. By Wednesday, it's gone. She didn't buy anything big. Small things. Snacks, data, rides, little transfers.
So she tries the fifty, thirty, twenty rule. Fifty percent for needs: rent, food, transport. Thirty percent for wants: going out, new clothes. Twenty percent for her future: savings and investing.
The trick? She pays her future first. The day the money lands, twenty percent moves to a separate account she doesn't touch.
Then she writes down every spend for one month. Just one month. She finds three hundred dollars a year going on things she didn't even remember buying.
A budget isn't a cage. It's a map. It shows you where the money goes, so you can choose where it goes.
