Hey, it's Kobo. Last time, Ada built a budget. Today, life tests it.
Her phone breaks. The repair costs four hundred dollars. Her friend Tunde had the same problem last year. He had no savings, so he borrowed. With interest, that four hundred became six hundred.
Ada doesn't borrow. She has an emergency fund.
An emergency fund is money you keep only for surprises. Job loss. Hospital. A broken phone. Not for sales, not for parties.
How much? A common target is three to six months of your basic expenses. If that sounds huge, start with one month. Then grow it.
Where? Somewhere safe and easy to reach, like a savings account or a money market fund. Not in crypto. Not in shares. Emergencies don't wait for the market to recover.
An emergency fund turns a crisis into an inconvenience.
