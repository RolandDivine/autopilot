Hey, it's Kobo. Let's talk about debt. Not all debt is the same.
Chidi, our chin chin king, borrows money to buy a second machine. The machine makes more chin chin than the loan costs. That's good debt. It pays for itself.
Emeka borrows on a quick loan app to buy new sneakers. Forty percent interest. The sneakers don't make him any money. That's bad debt. It eats tomorrow's income.
Here's a simple test. Will this debt help me earn more, or save more, than it costs? If not, think twice.
And watch out for two traps. One: minimum payments. Pay only the minimum on a card, and you can pay for years. Two: loan apps that charge huge fees and harass people who miss a payment.
If you're already in debt, list them all. Pay the most expensive interest first, while paying the minimum on the rest.
