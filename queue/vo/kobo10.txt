Hey, it's Kobo. Ada bought a little Bitcoin. Now, where does she keep it?
Option one: on the exchange where she bought it. Easy. But the exchange holds the keys. If it gets hacked or goes bust, like FTX in 2022, her coins can get stuck.
Option two: her own wallet. A wallet doesn't hold coins. It holds keys, the passwords that prove the coins are yours. When she creates one, she gets a seed phrase: twelve or twenty-four words.
Those words are everything. Whoever has them, has the money.
So, Kobo's rules. Write the seed phrase on paper. Never in a screenshot, never in the cloud, never in a chat. Keep it somewhere safe. And never, ever type it into a website or give it to anyone. No real support agent will ever ask for it.
Not your keys, not your coins. But also: lose your keys, lose your coins.
