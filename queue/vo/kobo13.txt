Hey, it's Kobo. Emeka put all his savings into one coin. A friend said it would go ten times. It went down ninety percent in a month.
Ada spreads hers out. Some in savings. Some in a fund that holds many companies. A small slice in crypto. If one falls, the others can hold her up.
That's diversification. Don't put all your eggs in one basket.
It works because different things move differently. When shares fall, government bonds sometimes rise. When one coin collapses, cash doesn't care.
Three questions before you invest. How much could I lose? What else do I own that moves the same way? And would I be okay if this went to zero?
Spreading out won't make you rich overnight. It keeps you in the game long enough to get there.
