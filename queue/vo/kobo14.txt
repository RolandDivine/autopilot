She starts with what she already knows. She's good with spreadsheets, so she offers to clean up small businesses' books on weekends.
Tunde loves cooking. He sells lunch packs to offices near his house. Femi turns his logo designs into templates people can buy again and again.
Notice the pattern. Each one sells a skill or a product, not a dream. No course that promises millions. No joining fee to sell something to your friends.
Kobo's tips. Start small, next to your job, not instead of it. Ask for payment upfront for new clients. Keep the side money in its own account, and set some aside for tax.
