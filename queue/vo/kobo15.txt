Scam one: the friendly stranger. A wrong-number message turns into weeks of chatting. Then they show you a crypto app where they're making a fortune. You deposit, you see profits, but you can never withdraw. It's fake.
Scam two: fake support. You post a problem online, and someone helping you asks for your seed phrase, or to share your screen. Real support never does that.
Scam three: the voice of family. An AI cloned voice calls, crying, asking for money urgently.
One defense beats them all: slow down. Scammers need you to hurry. Hang up. Call back on a number you already know. Never send money to someone you've only met online.
