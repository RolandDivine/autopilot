# Story bank

True stories from Roland, told in his words, for 17:00 stories and lesson hooks. Public repo: only add what he's happy to post.

Format: `- YYYY-MM-DD · topic · what happened (his words) · used: <date or no>`

- 2026-10-12 · crypto origin · 2019, 300 level at the University of Benin. Heard a friend talking about "trading digital assets", asked what it was because he's curious about new tech. His friend explained it; he researched it and got hooked on futures markets. As a student, the hook was extra income, money to do things he couldn't afford. It all clicked after heavy research: books, materials, following people, YouTube. It made sense fast because he'd already learned about the stock market from his grandpa. Seven years in now; it had its pitfalls, but he's glad he made the decision. · used: no
- 2026-10-12 · grandpa & first trade · His grandpa had a company. He showed young Roland the stock pages in the newspaper (that's how people checked prices then). He pointed to a Nigerian company (Roland doesn't remember the name) that had just listed an IPO and said he and his friends would likely get the stocks. Roland never traded stocks himself. His first crypto was Bitcoin, but it was too expensive per coin, so he bought Ethereum. Those were his first two coins. He lost a little at first, then had "beginner's luck" and made close to $500. · used: no
