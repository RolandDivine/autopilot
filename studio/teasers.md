# "Do you know…?" teasers (Roland, 15 Oct 2026)

One a day at 9 PM, Instagram feed post + the 9 PM story. A big "Do you know…?" question on a generated
image that opens a curiosity gap about tomorrow's 8 AM post. Rules:
- The promise must be kept: the next day's 8 AM post answers it directly. Log every teaser below and check
  this list before writing the next day's 8 AM post.
- Frame it as "Do you know why / Do you know you're…", aimed at a problem the reader recognises. Never insult
  the reader, never promise money or results, never tease something we can't deliver.
